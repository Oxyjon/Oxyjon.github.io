Play on Itch.io Play on Itch.io
Overview

You play as an individual in the midst of a financial crisis, due to past choices. Your circumstances lead you to turn to permaculture to pay off your debt and build a self-sustaining ecosystem in your apartment. Utilize your surroundings and an everchanging microclimate to cultivate crops while learning about real-world sustainable farming methods.
