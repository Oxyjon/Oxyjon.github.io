Architecting Robust Game Systems

Hi, I'm John Pelkman

I am a professional game programmer with commercial experience developing for both indie studios and high-scale live platforms like Unreal Engine For Fortnite (UEFN) and Unity.

My primary expertise lies in networking, systems programming, and gameplay architecture. Whether it's building 100-player synchronized quest loops, implementing cross-session player persistence, optimizing frame rates for console certification, or writing in-engine telemetry tools, I thrive in turning ambitious creative visions into performant, scalable realities.
