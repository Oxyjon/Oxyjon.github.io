Overview

T.I.M.E: A fast-paced 1v1 arena shooter set in a dystopian brutalist interior. Each round changes how you think about your actions! Featuring:
• Run-it-back: Sift through previous round events and decide where it all went wrong to rectify it next round!
• Dynamic Pick-Ups: Choose different power weapons and buffs every round.
• Frenetic Movement: Jetpack, slide, and bounce around to outmaneuver your opponent.
