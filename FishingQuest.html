Overview

Fishing Quest is a 100-player cozy fishing game set on a curious archipelago. Explore 10+ unique islands, reel in rare fish with dynamic difficulty mini-games, upgrade skills, and dive into engaging quests with townsfolk. Team up with fellow anglers, compare catches on leaderboards, and complete global community challenges.
