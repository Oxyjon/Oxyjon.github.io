Overview

EVO: Rogue is a thrilling 1-6 player roguelike featuring 25 rooms across 3 biomes with unique enemy archetypes. Battle with overpowered abilities and powerups like Poison, Lightning, and Health Steal. Earn coins by defeating creatures, unlock weapons and upgrades, and choose unique perks after each room. Cross-session progress is serialized so players can return to their upgrades anytime.
